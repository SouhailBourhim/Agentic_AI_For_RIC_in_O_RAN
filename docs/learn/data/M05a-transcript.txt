submit_intent(text)
    Submit the operator's intent, word for word (French or English), for example "Garantis 10 Mb/s au slice URLLC pendant 10 minutes." This only creates a request; it changes nothing on the network. The agent interprets it, the deterministic verifier checks it, and in demo mode an operator confirms it in a separate console. Returns the intent id and its first state ('received'): follow it with get_status.

get_kpis(slice, window_s)
    Recent KPIs per slice (throughput, PRB share, latency): mean, p95 and last value over the last window_s seconds of the cell's clock. slice is 'embb', 'urllc' or 'be'; omit it for all three. Read only.

get_status(intent_id)
    Where an intent stands: received, processing, interpreted, rejected (with the reason), awaiting_confirmation, applied, expired or cancelled; the objective it carries and whether it is in force. Without intent_id: the objective timetable and the recent intents. Read only.
